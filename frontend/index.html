Holiday Accommodation in Istria, Croatia

Palazzina Burjaki is a beautifully restored traditional Istrian stone house located in the peaceful village of Trgetari, near Labin and the popular beach resort of Rabac. This authentic Croatian holiday home offers the perfect blend of traditional architecture and modern comfort.

With 4 bedrooms, 5 bathrooms, and 235 m² of living space, our property accommodates up to 8 guests – ideal for families and groups seeking an unforgettable vacation in Istria, Croatia.
